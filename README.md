# framelad guide

Help for framelad, an RNG analysis emulator for Android.

### [Open the guide](https://twius.github.io/framelad-guide/)

## Getting started

1. Get framelad from the Play Store.
2. Open it and choose the folder your ROM backups are in.
3. Tap a ROM to load it.
4. Open the menu and go to Data. If the top row says `Set A`, `Set B` or
   `Set C`, you are ready to go.

## Notes

The app comes with no game data, so entities show as `#280` and dispositions as
`07`. You can add your own files to fix that, but you do not have to. Everything
works without them.

You supply your own ROM backups. None come with the app.

Nothing you do is sent anywhere. There are no accounts and no servers, and link
play uses your own wifi.
